Questions

How is my debt-free date calculated?

Each month, interest is added at your APR divided by 12, then your payment is subtracted. The calculator repeats this until the balance reaches zero.

Why does an extra payment save so much?

Extra money goes straight to principal, so less of your balance is charged interest every month after that.

What if my payment is too low?

If your payment is not larger than one month of interest, the balance never shrinks. The calculator warns you when this happens.
